# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want SpaceWheat's community — issues, pull requests, discussions, and any
other project space — to be welcoming and harassment-free for everyone,
regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in disagreements.
- Assume good faith; ask before assuming malice or incompetence.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully — this is a physics-heavy codebase and
  getting the details right matters more than being right the first time.
- Respect that this project is built partly through AI-agent collaboration;
  don't treat that as a reason to dismiss a contribution without reviewing it
  on its merits.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or reviews.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This applies within all project spaces (the repository, its issues and pull
requests) and when representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior by opening a private security advisory or
contacting the maintainer directly (see repository owner contact on GitHub).
All reports will be reviewed and investigated, and will result in a response
deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances. Maintainers who do not
follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary or
permanent repercussions.
