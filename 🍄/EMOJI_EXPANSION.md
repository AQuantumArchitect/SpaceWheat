Quest completion needs visual reward tiers beyond just resources.


## ⚠️ Caveats & Considerations

### Multi-Cultural Symbols
Religious/spiritual emojis (✝️☪️🕉️🔯) are included for:
- **Faction diversity** (89 factions need visual identity)
- **Sacred geometry** themes in quantum mechanics
- **No religious endorsement** - purely symbolic

**Alternative**: If controversial, replace with neutral symbols:
- ✝️ → ➕ (plus)
- ☪️ → 🌙⭐ (moon-star combo)
- 🕉️ → 🔱 (trident)
- 🔯 → ✡️ → ⬡ (hexagon)

### ZWJ Sequences
Some emojis may have variation selectors:
- ⏱️ (stopwatch with VS-16)
- 🌈 (may have VS-16)

Handle same as existing VS-16 pairs in manifest.

### Platform Support
All 67 emojis are Unicode 13.0+ standard:
- ✅ Supported by Twemoji
- ✅ Supported by OpenMoji
- ✅ No custom/private-use characters
