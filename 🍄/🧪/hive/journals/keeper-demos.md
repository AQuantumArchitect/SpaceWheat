## What I know

- The ▸ banner names the next key. `→` separates steps. `SHIFT [X]` / `CTRL+SHIFT [X]` mean hold
  those modifiers. The banner sometimes flips between two lines (see Walls).
- Hats (number row): Icon 5, Merchant 6, Captain 7, Ace 8, Operator 9, Druid 0. Re-pressing the hat
  you wear drops you back to Ace. Check `wearing_hat` before pressing a hat key. (I pressed 5 while
  already Icon and dropped to Ace; R became Strike and F became Fast-Fwd for a turn.)
- Ace: Q Gather (Shift Mass Gather, costs 🧺), E Pause, R Strike, F Explore / Fast-Fwd,
  Shift+F Mass Explore, Ctrl+Shift+F Reap Season (costs 🍼). Strike is refused until F explores.
- Icon: Q Trim Icon (needs 🐺), E Loops, R Add Icon / Track first / Incorporate (depends on the
  focused plot), F Track. The R label tells you the state: "Track first (F)", then "Ripening N%",
  then "Incorporate" when ripe.
- Ripening: after F tracks a plot, press `equal` (=) to speed the biome clock. A 20–35 s `wait`
  is usually enough. Ripening can dip (54% to 43%), so re-read R before pressing it.
- Druid: Q Spin −, E Superpose, R Spin +, F Play. Spin tips a plot toward a pole: Spin + toward
  the north glyph (first emoji of the plot), Spin − toward the south glyph.
- Operator: Shift+plot key marks a plot; R Build gate opens a picker; Q there is Bell.
- Plot keys G H J K L ; are the six plots of the biome you stand in. On the field T Y U I O switch
  biome (T TheDemos, Y Village, U StarterForest, I Woodlot, O FreshwaterSpring). Inside a menu the
  same keys switch tabs.
- X = story (T Self, Y Arc = open doors to Accept, U Charge = signed doors to Claim, I Guide,
  O Story). Accepting an Arc door signs it; Charge shows READY and R claims it.
- Claim sequence that worked: X, U, <plot key of the READY door>, R (for example X, U, J, R).
- Village loops for Master of the Mill: Icon hat, plot key, F track, = speed, wait, R incorporate.
  Each incorporation adds one loop. Master went from 51% to 80% to claimed over about 5 loops.
- C = board (Y Market = contracts to Accept, U Commitments = deliver held contracts).
  On the Market, F = Refresh, E = Inspect.
- ESC closes a menu. E inspects whatever is selected.
- `semicolon` is the ; key. `apostrophe` clears marks (Shift+plot marks; a plain plot key with an
  overlay open can also mark a plot, so clear with apostrophe after a stray mark).
- DANGER: with an overlay open, Y can open the PAUSED menu with SAVE SLOTS and [U] New. Do not
  press into it. ESC closes it. Check `screen_text` for "PAUSED" after each Y/X press.
