# The Boat Crisis — Quest Arc Fodder

## What happened (the substrate story)

The Quay Rooks have flooded the Harbor's manifest system.
Every small vessel (🛶) that enters Harbor gets logged, taxed, or hooked into a debt
instrument. The Rooks' manifest pressure (🛶→📋 Lindblad at 0.14, with ⚓→🪝 cross-qubit
at 0.10) has saturated their own 🛶 marginal to ~0.998 while collapsing the Salt-Runners'
to ~0.045. The Runners have salt cargo on the dock and no boats to move it.

Cascade consequences:
- **Sand supply dries up.** Boats were importing 🏜 to Harbor (🛶→🏜 Lindblad). No boats → Harbor Cargo qubit tips back toward 🧊 (ice). VolcanicFoundry's sand input drops.
- **Glass bottleneck.** VF's ⚡→🏜 glassmaking arc loses its substrate. 💎 production stalls.
- **Irrigation Jury is squeezed.** The Jury's water allocations reach Harbor via 💧 and 🪣. Without boats to distribute downstream, the system backs up. 🌾 in IJ starts decaying.
- **BioticFlux overpressures.** With nowhere to send its 🌾 output (IJ backs up → no delivery), the Harvest qubit saturates and the ecosystem tips toward 🍄 (fungal overgrowth — the death cycle dominates).

## The player sees

The Harbor's Transit qubit (🛶/🪝) reads: boats scarce, hooks dominant.
The IrrigationJury market shows: 💧 offered at low cost (water backing up with nowhere to go).
BioticFlux shows: 🌾=high and rising (wheat with no takers).
VolcanicFoundry shows: 🏜 marginal falling, 💎 stalling.
The SR⊗QR market: boats priced at 40+ (scarcity signal — spiky high reward for anyone who can move one).

## Three player paths

### Path A — Disrupt the manifest (help the Runners)

Invest in Salt-Runners' tradecraft axis (🔓/🔍). When 🔓 is high and 🔍 is active,
the Runners route boats through hidden channels that bypass Harbor's manifest system.
This reduces QR's effective ⚓→🪝 pump by introducing noise into their logging operation.

Mechanical: player pops Salt-Runners' 🔓 qubit repeatedly → 🔍 (informants) activates
via gated Lindblad → Runners' 🛶 begins recovering. Takes multiple pops and sustained
attention. The Rooks will respond (their 💰→🪝 gated term tries to re-hook escaped vessels).

Story beat: "You've found the passage they don't map. The Runners know it too — they just
needed someone to hold the manifest officer's attention."

### Path B — Legitimize the route (help the Irrigation Jury)

The Jury has legal authority over water channels (🏗 Imposed). If the player invests in IJ's
⚖ (Judgment) qubit — pops it toward high occupancy, builds standing with the Jury — the Jury
can issue a channel ruling that exempts specific water routes from Harbor's manifest requirement.

Mechanical: pop IJ's ⚖ qubit → ⚖ gates 🌱 growth (cross-axis coupling) → when 🌱 is high and
⚖ is high, IJ issues a "water-right ruling" (quest completion). The ruling creates a temporary
Lindblad bypass: 💧→🛶 at Harbor increases, weakening QR's manifest grip on that qubit.

This path is slower and requires building IJ standing. But it's durable — a legal ruling
persists until the Rooks challenge it (future quest hook).

Story beat: "The Jury has ruled. Water flows where justice wills. The Rooks' manifests don't
cover the irrigation channels — and suddenly, neither do the boats."

### Path C — Buy out the cartel (leverage the money bridge)

Village has 🧺/💰 as a qubit. QuayRooks have 💰/🪝. The money bridge (V⊗QR market) means
the player can use Village economic output to influence QR's 💰 marginal.

When QR's 💰 marginal drops (player absorbs it via contracts), their 💰→🪝 gated term weakens
— fewer hooks get set. The Rooks aren't defeated; they're just temporarily satisfied with cash
instead of vessel control.

This path is fast but expensive and temporary. The Rooks re-accumulate 💰 from ship traffic
(🚢→💰 Lindblad at 0.18) within ~30-60 seconds and reassert manifest pressure.

Story beat: "The Rooks are satisfied — for now. But you've shown them what the boats are worth
in coin, which means they'll charge more next time."

## Downstream rewards (if boats are restored)

- Harbor Cargo qubit (🧊/🏜) tips back toward 🏜 (sand shipping resumes)
- VolcanicFoundry 🏜 marginal recovers → 💎 production resumes
- IrrigationJury 🌾 marginal stabilizes → BioticFlux Harvest qubit re-balances
- SR⊗QR boat price drops from 40+ toward 5-8 (normal contested-crossing range)
- New market opens: VF produces 💎 → creates demand signal for the Spaceport (next arc)

## Connected quest hooks

- **The Spaceport wants glass** — VF cannot deliver 💎 while the sand bottleneck persists.
  Resolving the boat crisis is a prerequisite for unlocking the Spaceport supply chain.
- **The Jury's water-right ruling** (Path B) creates a faction standing effect with IJ that
  persists. High IJ standing later unlocks canal-building mechanics that give SR permanent
  alternative routes.
- **The Rooks remember Path C** — if the player buys out the cartel, QR's subsequent contract
  prices for Village⊗QR commerce are elevated (debt hook applies to the player now too).

## Substrate notes for quest implementation

The boat crisis is not a scripted event — it is the natural steady state of the substrate
when QR and SR coexist at Harbor under current Lindblad rates. The player does not trigger it;
they discover it by reading the Harbor market and noticing the price anomaly.

Quest completion condition: Harbor Transit qubit (🛶 marginal) > 0.40 sustained for 30s.
This corresponds to the contested crossing being genuinely balanced rather than Rooks-dominant.
