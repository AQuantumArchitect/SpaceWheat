### Where the other 88% is not

The first pass at #528 read `avg_batch_time_ms` as the frame. The second
divided native ms by a clamped clock. This pass writes the shares into the
report (`attribution`) from wall-clock snapshot deltas:

| seam | share of wall (endgame capped) |
|---|---|
| native `evolve_all_lookahead` | 12.0% |
| consume + buffer-state | 1.6% |
| refill / queue setup | 0.3% |
| merge | 0.2% |
| poll | ~0% |
| accounted | 14.2% |
| unaccounted | 85.8% |

So: not the GPU, not Eigen, not the GDScript unpack, not the refill walk.
The next instrument belongs on viz `_process` (force graph, bubbles, farm
tick) and the other autoloads. Do not wire `submit_lookahead_job` on the
strength of this number — moving 12% off the main thread will not remove a
150 ms tail whose 86% is somewhere else.

`TIME_PROCESS` ~90 ms and `TIME_PHYSICS_PROCESS` ~40 ms still show up in the
1 Hz snapshots. Those are last-frame samples and land on the slow frames;
they are not a share of the 9 ms median. Use `attribution`.
