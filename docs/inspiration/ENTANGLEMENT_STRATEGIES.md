# Entanglement Strategies & Bell State Gameplay

Bell states as distinct playstyles. Entanglement infrastructure that survives harvest.

## Bell States as Playstyles

| State | Character | Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| |Φ+⟩ (same) | Synchronized | Both plots give same outcome; double production |
| |Ψ+⟩ (opposite) | Complementary | One gives wheat, one gives moon; two resources per harvest |
| |Φ-⟩ (same, phase) | Amplified | Phase kickback boosts energy of both |
| |Ψ-⟩ (opposite, phase) | Conversion | Measuring one converts the other to opposite state |

## Entanglement Infrastructure

Key UX insight: **entanglement infrastructure survives harvest.**

Two layers:
1. **Quantum entanglement** — temporary, collapses on measurement
2. **Physical infrastructure** (pipes, couplings, gates) — persistent, survives harvests

Building a Bell pair coupling between two plots is infrastructure.
Like building a mill. You place it once; it applies every future planting.
The player gradually wires up their farm like a quantum circuit board.

## Energy Amplification via Entanglement

Two entangled plots grow together (multiplicative energy accumulation).
Precise harvest timing required: measure at peak of synchronized evolution
for the amplification bonus.

Sun/moon Hamiltonian pushes both theta values in sync → amplifying effect
builds over multiple phrames.

## Cross-Biome Entanglement (Future)

Majorana bridge between two biomes → information stored nonlocally.
Measuring plot A in Biome 1 influences outcome of plot B in Biome 2.
No coupling overhead; topologically protected.

Use case: synchronized harvesting across distant biomes.
The player who understands entanglement can farm at galactic scale.
