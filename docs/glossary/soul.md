The **soul** is the player, read as physics: a density matrix ρ over the same
12-qubit concept space the [factions](faction.md) live in — each faction a
basis ket of twelve chosen poles (material↔mystical, common↔elite,
instant↔eternal…), and you a superposition of all of them. The
[enclave](enclave.md) forbids its *world* from decaying; it makes one
exception, and the exception is you.

**Purity is who you are.** Tr(ρ²) = 1 means a committed identity; the mixed
floor (≈ 0.011 over ~90 factions) means a life not yet chosen. The game
speaks it in four bands — *resolved*, *leaning*, *torn*, *smeared across many
selves* — and whispers when you cross one, in the voice of whichever faction
holds the most of you.

**Only learning moves the committed mass.** Each new icon you incorporate
pumps its owning faction — and everyone who speaks its poles — so learning
inside one tradition resolves you, and learning across traditions spreads
you. Coherences between your possible selves fade on their own (τ = 300 s),
like [fading](fading.md), but the diagonal — what you have actually chosen —
stays until a new choice moves it. The Bath eats phase; only decisions eat
commitment.

**Kinship** is the soul's [resonance](resonance.md): where resonance reads
how a faction sits with a *biome's* live state, kinship reads how a faction
sits with *you* — the per-axis agreement between your dominant mode and
their live alignment, printed on every contract you inspect (*kin, warm,
acquainted, distant, opposed*).

Read yourself back anywhere: **M → Eigenstate** shows the purity band, the
axes you are becoming, and who holds you; factions rank themselves by overlap
with your principal state on the same card. Some doors in the story open only
for a resolved soul — the island eventually stops asking questions of anyone
who has answered them.
