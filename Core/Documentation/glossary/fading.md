**Fading** is the wet country's ambient verb: the loss of quantum character to the
[Bath](bath.md). Dephasing first — coherence bleeding out through channels no
population can see (T₂) — then relaxation, population following it down (T₁). The
first fading the player ever witnesses is the reveal of a sense organ the closed
game never let fire: purity is size, coherence is color, and both were frozen at
maximum for the entire enclave age. **The world goes gray while nothing moves.**

Fading is *managed*, not fought:

- **Zeno**: repeated measurement pins a state against the drain — watching keeps.
- **Pattern**: topological protection ([invariant](invariant.md)) — Lanternfall's
  edge mode holds while its bulk grays, because the winding is not the Bath's to
  change.
- **Interference**: dark states stand in shapes the drain cannot see.
- **The rite**: what fades is not gone — it pools as entropy, and the wet-country
  reap pays kT·ΔS from the season's accumulated loss.

The [berry](berry.md) register already knew: below coherence ε the loop's path
integration freezes — a faded state has no well-defined walk. In the enclave that
guard never triggered. Out here, ripeness must beat the gray to the harvest.
