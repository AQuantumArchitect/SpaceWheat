The **Bath** is the environment of the open world — in physics, the reservoir a
quantum system couples to; in the game, the patient fact that in **wet country**
everything that touches anything leaks into it. It is not a monster and not a wall.
It has no malice and no mercy. It is uniform, inexhaustible, and incapable of
stopping — the [enclave](enclave.md)'s exact opposite and its definition by contrast.

Mechanically the Bath is the Lindblad generator, running live wherever a biome's
regime is open (the What Fades seam, `docs/OPEN_CAMPAIGN.md`): the
[webway](webway.md)'s channels flowing, sinks drinking, pumps feeding, phase
[fading](fading.md) before population. Of the world's 162 biomes, 64 author real
Lindblad structure — the Bath's country. The other 98 never wrote a leak and stay
coherent by nature: enclaves born, not built.

What the player learns about the Bath, chapter by chapter:

- **It eats phase first** (T₂ before T₁): the world goes gray while nothing moves.
- **Watching keeps** (Zeno): measurement pins what it touches — the player's oldest
  verb becomes their shield.
- **It digs basins**: with contraction, attractors stop being metaphors. The
  compass needle becomes destiny.
- **It can be blinded**: dark states — superpositions arranged so the paths into
  the drain cancel — stand where the Bath's eyes pass through.
- **It can be farmed**: the rite pays kT·ΔS from a season's accumulated
  dissipation. The Bath is not only an appetite. Managed, it is a garden.
